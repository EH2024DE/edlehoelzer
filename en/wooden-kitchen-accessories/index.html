Start with what you use daily

A substantial cutting board provides a working surface. A smaller snack board serves breakfast or an evening meal. A spatula turns food, while a dough scraper gathers chopped ingredients or handles dough.

Read more

Choose a juice groove if you regularly work with juicy food and the board is suitable. It is an option with a purpose, not a detail every board needs. Size, weight and available storage should guide your choice.

Quality is more than the name of the wood

Look at the surface, edges, joints and handling. A tool should feel comfortable, have a suitable construction and fit the work it is meant to do.

Read more

Colour and grain make each piece individual, but they do not replace careful workmanship. We select timber and check the finished pieces in our workshop. If you are unsure, we can help you compare suitable products.

Care belongs to the collection

Wash wooden tools by hand, wipe them dry and let air circulate around them. Appropriate care refreshes a dry surface and supports long-term use.

Read more

Our care balm combines linseed-oil varnish and regional beeswax. Check the individual product page for its price and what is included; not every board or accessory automatically includes a tin.

A gift that has a place in the kitchen

Choose a familiar daily ritual rather than an anonymous decorative object. A board, dough scraper or spatula can be personalised with an individually agreed engraving.

Questions before you choose

Which accessories are useful?

Start with your cooking habits: a suitable board and one or two tools you will regularly use.

Do wooden tools need complicated care?

No. Hand washing, drying and occasional suitable surface care are the essentials. Avoid the dishwasher and prolonged soaking.

Can I combine matching woods?

Yes, subject to the current selection. We can help you choose complementary pieces.
