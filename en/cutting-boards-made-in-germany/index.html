From timber to a considered construction

We source wood, record the stock, inventory and store it, and allow controlled drying. We then select and cut suitable pieces and determine the construction for the intended board.

Read more

Material, machine work and handwork belong together. We discuss a particular wood's source where relevant rather than presenting every piece as having the same regional origin.

Shape, surface and finish

We work the faces, shape and functional details, then sand and finish the board. The edge, grip and feel are considered together. Our boards are traditionally made by hand, deliberately without CNC.

Read more

The wood species alone does not prove quality. Construction, workmanship and the finished piece need to be judged together. We explain material choices and the origin of the wood as specifically as we can.

Before a board leaves the workshop

We check the finished piece, photograph it naturally, then pack and dispatch it. Each board receives another close look before shipping.

Read more

Our preparation includes a fresh fine sanding where appropriate, linseed-oil finish and our care balm. The care guide explains our process and the difference between workshop preparation and routine care at home.

A workshop relationship beyond the purchase

Custom boards begin with a discussion about the kitchen and intended use. Existing boards may be suitable for refurbishment, subject to their condition. We can help you decide which next step makes sense.

Questions before you choose

Where do you make the boards?

In our workshop in Central Hesse, Germany.

Is all the wood from the same region?

No. We discuss the source and availability of the wood for the actual product rather than making one blanket origin claim.

Is all wood FSC certified?

We do not claim that every piece is FSC certified. Ask about the specific material and available evidence.

Can a used board be refurbished?

Where the substance and condition are suitable, we can assess it for refurbishment. The service is currently offered within Germany.
