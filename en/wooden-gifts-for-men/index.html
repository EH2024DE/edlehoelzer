For the way he cooks

A regular home cook may appreciate a practical preparation board. For someone who enjoys BBQ, consider carving and serving space. A host may prefer a piece that moves easily from kitchen to table.

Read more

Consider the recipient's worktop, storage and ability to lift the board. Bigger is not automatically more thoughtful. A smaller tool that is used often can be the better gift.

Make it personal without overloading it

A name, date or short message can be engraved where the product is suitable. We agree the design and position with you and prepare a mock-up before making it.

Leave room for the working area and any juice groove. If several individual pieces of a wood are available, ask us about choosing a favourite grain.

A gift that stays useful

A wooden piece should fit a real routine, whether the occasion is a birthday, housewarming, wedding or Christmas. Check the product's contents, availability and delivery information before ordering.

Questions before you choose

What makes a good wooden gift?

A piece suited to the recipient's habits, space and preferred feel, rather than size or decoration alone.

Can the gift be engraved?

Yes, where the piece and motif are suitable. The layout is agreed before production.

Is a board a suitable BBQ gift?

It can be, if its working area, weight and handling suit the way the recipient grills and serves.
