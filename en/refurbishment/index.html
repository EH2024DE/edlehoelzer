Refurbish or buy new?

A new board from us starts at 60 €. A refresh starts at 41 €, full restoration at 95 € – each including return shipping within Germany. If your board is stable and the wood still has enough substance, refurbishment is often the more sensible choice.

Solid wood may age. It may carry marks. But it should not be given up before it is truly at the end.

Proof of solid wood

Not every marked board is finished.

Deep knife marks, dry patches or a dull surface do not automatically mean replacement. If enough material remains, a board can be sanded, oiled and used again.

How it works

  1. Send photos Send pictures of the front, back, edges and worn areas. Add rough size, wood species and how the board has been used.
  2. Honest assessment We tell you which package makes sense, or whether care at home is enough. For full restoration, we confirm the price after photos.
  3. Send the board You send the board to our workshop in Central Hesse using your preferred carrier. Pack it well so it arrives safely.
  4. Receive it back We refurbish it and ship it back carefully packed. Return shipping within Germany is included in each fixed price.

When we say no

Not every board can be saved in a clean or economically sensible way. Cracks that run through the whole board, strong warping or hygienically problematic damage may be beyond repair – and we say that clearly.

The photo assessment before any order is mandatory. That keeps the process clear for both sides.

FAQ

Common questions

When is a refresh enough, and when does a board need full restoration?

A refresh is for stable boards with fine knife marks, dull areas or a dry surface. Full restoration is for cracks, deep dents, loose or missing feet, or a strongly uneven surface. If in doubt, send photos first.

Is return shipping included?

Yes. Return shipping within Germany is included in each fixed price. You cover the shipping to our workshop.

Can you refurbish a board that was not made by Edle Hölzer?

Yes, as long as it is a solid wood board and its condition allows a useful refurbishment. Send photos and we will take a look.

How long does refurbishment take?

A refresh usually takes 3–5 working days after the board arrives. Full restoration usually takes 5–8 working days, depending on the work needed.

What happens if the board cannot be saved?

We tell you honestly after the photo assessment, before you send the board. In that case, there are no costs for you.
