View another board When a groove makes sense

For the moment when juice leaves the food

When steak rests, tomatoes are sliced or fruit is served, liquid appears. A cleanly routed juice groove keeps more of it on the board and less of it on the counter or table.

That makes the moment calmer, especially when food is carved or served in front of guests.

Useful detail, not decoration

A juice groove needs enough board thickness and enough remaining cutting surface. If the board is too small, the groove can take away more space than it gives back.

That is why groove, format and use case should be considered together.

When no groove is better

For bread, dry prep or a very minimal work board, a flat surface can be more practical and easier to wipe clean.

FAQ

Common questions

Can the board be engraved?

Yes, if the wood, size and placement make sense. Short names, dates or initials usually work best.

How do I care for a solid wood board?

Wipe it clean, let it dry freely and refresh the surface with a thin layer of suitable oil or balm when it looks dry.

Is end grain better for knives?

End grain is especially kind to knives because the blade moves between upright fibres instead of cutting across them.

Can I request a custom size?

Yes. Send a short inquiry with size, use case and wood preference, and we will check what is sensible.
