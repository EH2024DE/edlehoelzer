What matters in use

A board should fit the way it is handled every day. Size, weight, wood species and surface finish matter more than a loud product name.

We keep the construction honest: solid wood, clean sanding, oil and wax instead of a sealed plastic-like surface.

Wood, shape and feel

Oak feels bright and marked, walnut is darker and calmer, larch can feel warmer and more rustic. End grain brings more presence, edge grain keeps things lighter.

The best choice is the one that will actually be used, cleaned and enjoyed often.

Care and ageing

Wood should not go into the dishwasher and should not be soaked. Wipe it clean, let it dry freely and refresh it with a thin layer of suitable oil or balm when the surface feels dry.

Used well, a solid wood board develops patina instead of becoming disposable.

FAQ

Common questions

Can the board be engraved?

Yes, if the wood, size and placement make sense. Short names, dates or initials usually work best.

How do I care for a solid wood board?

Wipe it clean, let it dry freely and refresh the surface with a thin layer of suitable oil or balm when it looks dry.

Is end grain better for knives?

End grain is especially kind to knives because the blade moves between upright fibres instead of cutting across them.

Can I request a custom size?

Yes. Send a short inquiry with size, use case and wood preference, and we will check what is sensible.
