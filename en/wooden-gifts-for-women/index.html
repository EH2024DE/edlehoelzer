Choose for a real daily ritual

For someone who bakes, a wooden dough scraper can be a useful companion. A breakfast board or butter knife suits a different routine. Cooking and sharing food may suggest a preparation or serving board.

Read more

Use those habits as starting points, not assumptions. The format, weight, grain and storage space should fit the person and her kitchen.

A personal choice of wood and detail

Wood varies naturally in colour and grain. If several examples are available, we can help choose a favourite piece. A restrained engraving adds a personal detail where suitable.

Read more

We discuss the text, motif and position and prepare a mock-up before making the engraving. A clear, well-placed detail usually works better than a design that takes over the working area.

Something to keep using

A birthday, wedding, housewarming or Christmas gift can become part of daily life rather than remaining decoration. Read the individual product page for the actual contents, availability and care guidance.

Questions before you choose

Which wood should I choose?

Look at the actual grain and colour alongside the recipient's preferred feel and kitchen. We can help compare suitable pieces.

Is personalisation possible?

Yes, with an individually agreed engraving where the product and motif are suitable.

Does the photographed set always come together?

No. Check the specific product page for what is included and ask us if you want to combine items.
