From the workshop

A dough scraper that works with you

A small tool that earns its place on the worktop every time you bake.

Anyone who bakes sourdough regularly knows the moment: the dough sticks, flour covers the worktop and a thin plastic scraper bends as soon as a firm wheat dough pushes back. A solid wood dough scraper behaves differently. It stays stable, sits calmly in the hand and guides the dough with a carefully sanded edge.

Our oak dough scrapers are made in the workshop in Staufenberg, Central Hesse. Özgür shapes every piece by hand. The outline is cut, the edges are sanded in several stages and the surface is finished with food-safe oil. The result is not a perfectly identical factory product. Grain, colour and weight follow the piece of wood.

What makes a useful dough scraper?

The edge matters first. It needs to be fine enough to lift bread dough cleanly from the worktop, but it should never become sharp or fragile. A gently rounded sanding profile protects the table and gives enough control to divide a firm dough or slide under a shaped loaf.

Material matters as well. The scraper is made from solid wood without a plastic coating. Oak is robust and has a reassuring weight in the hand. Depending on availability, other species such as walnut or cherry may be offered. For anyone looking for a wooden bench scraper or lasting sourdough equipment, it is a tool designed for repeated use rather than quick replacement.

Sourdough gives the scraper several jobs. After bulk fermentation it helps release dough from the bowl, divide larger batches and collect sticky remains from the surface. During preshaping, the broad edge helps create tension without adding unnecessary flour. Soft rye and wheat doughs become easier to control when the tool has a firm, comfortable body.

A flexible plastic scraper still has its place with very wet doughs. The wooden scraper does not have to replace every specialised tool. It adds stability and a warmer feel for everyday bread making, and it also works for portioning rolls, moving chopped ingredients or cleaning flour from the worktop.

Care is straightforward: remove dough with a little water, dry the scraper and let it air freely. It does not belong in the dishwasher and should never soak. When the surface looks dry after extended use, apply a thin layer of suitable wood care. Our care guide explains the process.

For folding, dividing, shaping and tidying the worktop, a handcrafted dough scraper always has the same purpose: it makes the work more controlled without becoming the centre of attention. For people who bake often, that makes it a dependable kitchen tool rather than a decorative extra.
