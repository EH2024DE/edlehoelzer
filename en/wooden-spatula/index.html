A considered tool for your pan

A wooden spatula works without a metal edge against the pan. Smoothly finished surfaces and a length of around 30 cm give it a comfortable feel and keep your hand away from the hot pan.

Read more

Use it without unnecessary pressure, including on coated pans. Check edges regularly for rough spots. Wood is a solid material, not a flexible plastic blade, and should not be used as a lever.

Clean briefly. Let it dry freely.

Wash by hand after cooking, wipe dry and allow air to circulate around the tool. Do not put it in the dishwasher or leave it soaking.

Read more

If the surface begins to feel dry or rough, suitable wood care can refresh it. Our care guide explains cleaning, drying and applying a thin coat without leaving excess on the surface.

Build a small, useful kitchen collection

Pair a spatula with a wooden dough scraper or butter knife in a complementary wood. Start with tools you will actually use rather than collecting unnecessary accessories.

Questions before you choose

Can I choose an individual spatula?

If more than one example of a wood is available, ask us for photos before dispatch.

Is it dishwasher safe?

No. Brief hand washing, drying with a cloth and free air drying are the appropriate routine.

Are all woods always available?

No. Check the current Etsy listing for the woods and versions available to order.
