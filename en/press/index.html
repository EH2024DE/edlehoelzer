Press and people behind Edle Hölzer

Edle Hölzer is a small woodcraft workshop in Central Hesse. We make boards and kitchen tools with individual grain, direct personal advice and a focus on keeping good pieces in use.

Contact us for press enquiries

Özgür: design, timber and craftsmanship

Özgür Koç founded Edle Hölzer and has worked in skilled crafts for around 20 years. He is responsible for design, wood selection and production.

Read more

The boards are traditionally worked by hand, deliberately without CNC. The material, intended use, edges, surface and handling are considered together rather than judged by wood species alone.

Serkan: making the details visible

Serkan Koç is an engineer and amateur photographer, responsible for the online presence. Through photography and video he brings the craft, materials and details of the work into view.

Stories from the workshop

Care instead of disposal, choosing material for a purpose and working with historic timber are recurring themes. The numbered Erbstück series gives selected old wood another role.

Read more

Not every old beam is suitable for a cutting board. We assess the substance, cracks and grain before deciding whether to make a piece. Existing traces can remain part of its character; suitability comes first.

A digital space for personal advice

The website helps people compare formats, learn about care and consider a board in their own kitchen. Custom projects begin with a conversation about use, dimensions and budget.
