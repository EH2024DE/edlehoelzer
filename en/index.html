About us

The people behind the craft.

Hi, I'm Özgür.

I founded Edle Hölzer, have worked in skilled crafts for around 20 years and am responsible for design, wood selection and production.

In our workshop in Central Hesse, we make our boards by hand using traditional methods, deliberately without CNC.

Hi, I'm Serkan.

I'm an engineer and amateur photographer, responsible for the online presence of Edle Hölzer.

Through photography and video, I bring Özgür's craftsmanship, the materials and the details of our work into view.
