What solid wood means

The working surface is made from real wood rather than a thin decorative veneer. A board may be made from a single piece or carefully joined elements. Edge-grain and end-grain constructions show the material in different ways.

Read more

Sufficient material can allow future sanding and refurbishment where the condition is suitable. Solid wood is not indestructible: moisture, heat, cracks and failed joints still need appropriate assessment.

Match the format to everyday use

For daily preparation, leave enough clear working space. A breakfast or snack board can be smaller and easier to carry. A substantial chopping block is better suited to remaining on the worktop.

Read more

A juice groove, grip recesses, engraving or a custom format each serve a different purpose. Choose useful details rather than assuming the largest or most heavily equipped board is the best one.

Care and repair

Wash briefly by hand, wipe dry and let both sides release moisture. Do not use the dishwasher or leave the board soaking. Refresh a dry surface with suitable care.

Read more

For a rough or deeply scratched board, ask whether sanding or workshop refurbishment is appropriate. Surface care does not repair a structural crack or an opening joint.

Questions before you choose

Is solid wood always one single piece?

No. Joined solid wood elements are also solid wood. The construction and workmanship matter.

What thickness should I choose?

Consider size, weight and handling together. A thicker board can offer substance but is also heavier.
