Made for real kitchens

Our completed projects are starting points, not a fixed catalogue of sizes. Oak and Yellow Poplar show how different woods can change the appearance of a substantial board. We choose suitable timber together, based on use, budget and availability.

Read more

A custom board does not have to be XXL. We can also discuss a smaller serving board, matching breakfast boards for your family or a holder made for a board you already own. Tell us what you want to use it for before deciding on the details.

Dimensions, material and handling

Measure the usable worktop, including the space you need around the board. Consider the sink, hob, cupboards and where you will lift or store it. Thickness, construction and wood affect the weight as well as the appearance.

Read more

We discuss edge grain or end grain, rounded or bevelled edges, grip recesses, a stop edge and a juice groove where suitable. Engraving needs a position that works with the design and leaves the working area practical. Not every combination is sensible; we will say so openly.

A realistic price framework

Depending on size and specification, custom pieces can start at a few hundred euros. We calculate your board individually; there is no universal price per centimetre.

Read more

Our 75 × 60 cm XXL reference board with a stop edge starts at €1,199 and can reach around €1,600–1,700 depending on specification. This is an upper-range example, not a minimum budget or the quoted price of the photographed customer projects.

From first idea to your finished board

Send your intended use and any rough dimensions. We discuss feasibility, wood, a price range and production time. We make the agreed design only after your approval.

Read more

A kitchen photo can help, but you do not need one for the first enquiry. If you are unsure about wood or construction, leave those choices open. We agree collection or dispatch as part of the project; a custom build does not automatically have the dispatch time of a ready-made product.

Questions before you choose

Can I order any size?

We check whether the proposed dimensions, thickness and construction are suitable. Available timber, stability, weight and everyday handling all matter.

Can you add a juice groove or engraving?

Yes, where the board and intended use allow it. We agree the position and design before production.

Does an enquiry place an order?

No. We first discuss your idea, feasibility, budget and timing. Production requires your approval.

Tell us about your idea

Your first enquiry is free and does not place an order. We discuss feasibility, budget and timing with you.
