When does a custom cutting board make sense?

A custom board makes sense when the place, size or use is more important than choosing a standard product. This can be a kitchen island, a grill station, a serving ritual or a gift with specific dimensions.

SizeThe board should fit where it will actually be used and cleaned.
WoodOak, walnut, end grain or available material each behave differently.
DetailsJuice groove, handle grooves or engraving only make sense when they fit the use.
WeightBigger is not automatically better if the board becomes hard to handle.

What Edle Hölzer checks before making it

We look at use case, format, wood, thickness, cleaning, storage and the requested details. The goal is not the largest possible board, but a wooden piece that will be used regularly.

When a custom board is not the best choice

If you need a very light board, dishwasher-safe material or a product available immediately, an existing Etsy listing may be more practical. Shipping options and final checkout are shown on Etsy for available products.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I request any size?

Not every size is practical or technically sensible. We check size, use and material before confirming a custom board.

Can a custom board include a juice groove?

Yes, if the size and thickness allow it. A juice groove is useful for meat, fruit and BBQ, but not necessary for every board.

Can I request engraving?

Yes. Short names, initials, dates or simple logos work best. Placement should fit how the board will be used.

How does checkout work?

Available products are purchased through Etsy. For custom boards, send an inquiry first so material, format and feasibility can be checked.
