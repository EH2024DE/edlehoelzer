When does engraving make sense?

Engraving is strongest when it supports the material instead of covering it. It works well for weddings, birthdays, housewarming gifts, family pieces or small corporate gifts.

Good ideasName, initials, date, family name, short line or simple symbol.
Use mattersOn working boards, engraving should avoid the main cutting area when possible.
Logo checksCompany logos need enough contrast and simplicity to work on wood.
Quiet placementThe wood should remain the main impression.

When engraving is not the right choice

Very long messages, highly detailed artwork or engraving across the whole surface can make a board harder to use and visually weaker. If the board is meant for heavy daily cutting, restrained placement is usually better.

FAQ

Common questions

What can be engraved on a cutting board?

Names, initials, dates, family names, short messages or simple logos usually work best.

Can every board be engraved?

Many boards can be engraved, but not every position is sensible. Working boards need careful placement.

Is an engraved cutting board a good wedding gift?

Yes, if the engraving stays restrained and the board remains useful.

Can a company logo be engraved?

Often yes. We check whether the logo is suitable for wood and readable at the requested size.
