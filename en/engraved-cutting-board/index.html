Cutting boards with engraving

A name, a date or a clear motif can turn a useful board into a personal gift. We help you find a position that belongs on the board, not in the way of using it.

Find a board for my engraving

Keep the message clear

Short names, dates, lettering and simple motifs usually work best. Light wood often makes an engraving more visible than dark or strongly figured timber.

Read more

Tell us the occasion and the result you have in mind. For business logos, the clarity of the artwork and the available space matter more than the number of details. We check whether a motif is suitable instead of promising that every file will work.

Choose the position together

An edge, corner, handle area, underside or dedicated serving area may suit the design. The right position depends on the board and its intended use.

Read more

An engraving in a frequently used cutting area will be exposed to knife marks. A juice groove and engraving also need space to coexist. We discuss these details and provide a mock-up before making the engraving.

A personal detail, not a separate product

Choose your board first, then agree the engraving. Cutting-board engraving costs €10; the available options are shown on the relevant product page.

Read more

For dough scrapers, spatulas and butter knives, send your text or artwork and discuss it with us. The product photos show examples; they are not a promise that every layout suits every piece.

From idea to approval

Send your chosen product and wording or motif. We discuss legibility and placement, prepare the mock-up and make the engraving after you approve it.

Questions before you choose

Can every board be engraved?

In principle we can engrave wooden boards, but we check the material, available space, motif and intended use first.

Can you engrave a company logo?

Yes, if the artwork and scale are suitable. We discuss the layout before making it.

Is a bigger engraving better?

Not necessarily. A clear, well-positioned engraving often fits a board better than a large design in the working area.
