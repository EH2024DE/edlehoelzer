Three lives. One Erbstück.

It once supported a house. Now it carries your dinner.

A cutting board made from historic oak. With beech lines that can tell your story.

Origin

First, there was a tree.

Oak grows slowly. Timber destined to support a house had already grown for decades before it became a beam in a timber-frame building. There it stayed.

Residents came and went. The beam remained.

Today we recover such beams from timber-frame buildings in our region, sometimes ourselves. Not every beam becomes a board. We assess its substance, cracks and grain. If the material is unsuitable, it does not become an Erbstück.

Selected historic timber becomes a cutting board. Its existing marks remain part of its character.

The foundation stays. Life leaves its marks.

For us, oak stands for the substance of our lives. Beech belongs to every Erbstück. Its light lines interrupt the surface like the moments that shape us: milestones, encounters, setbacks or a new beginning.

Their number and arrangement can hold your own story. There is no fixed code: you decide what the lines mean to you.

We agree the design together and work the beech in by hand, without CNC machinery. The format and substance of the historic oak determine what is possible.

Its story continues with you.

From tree to timber-frame beam, from a building to your kitchen: this wood has a story that began long before you. You add new marks and memories. Perhaps one day you will pass it on, so the next generation can tell their own story with it.

An Erbstück is not something to hide. Between shared meals it deserves a place in your kitchen. That is why a matching stand is included: to display it and look forward to the next memory.

What accompanies your Erbstück

A matching stand, care balm and care instructions are always included. Every Erbstück carries its own number. For beams from our own dismantling projects, a wooden Erbstück card records the town or district of origin in Central Hesse.

Made to be passed on.

  • At least one side is 30 cm long.

    The other dimensions follow the beam. Not every requested format is possible.

  • At least 4 cm thick.

    Made for use and for passing on. The thickness leaves material for later resurfacing, with the aim of allowing future generations to continue their story with the board.

  • The marks remain.

    Colour variations, grain and suitable historical features shape the board.

  • Each piece is unique.

    Once the timber has been worked, it cannot be ordered again identically.

Questions about your Erbstück

Can I choose the dimensions?

Within the possibilities of the wood: at least one side is 30 cm and thickness is at least 4 cm. We agree the remaining dimensions together. Whether later resurfacing is possible depends on the board’s condition.

Can I shape the beech lines?

Yes. We agree the number and arrangement with you. The lines can stand for people or moments important to you. We work them in by hand; the oak determines what is possible.

What does the wooden card tell me?

For timber from our own dismantling projects, it records the former town or district. No private addresses or precise GPS locations are published.

Can I add an engraving?

Depending on the piece, yes. We send you a mockup before engraving and wait for your approval.

How do I care for it?

Clean by hand and dry thoroughly. Do not soak or put it in a dishwasher. Care balm and instructions accompany your Erbstück.
