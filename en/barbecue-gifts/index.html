Choose for the food and the cook

A steak lover, smoker owner and host preparing food for a group do not necessarily need the same board. Match the working area to the food and leave enough room for carving and serving.

Read more

Large cuts can benefit from a substantial surface, but a board that is difficult to carry or wash may be the wrong gift. Consider worktop space, weight, grip and where it will be stored.

What makes a useful Pitmaster Board?

A stable working surface, appropriate dimensions and comfortable handling matter more than decorative extras. A juice groove can help with juicy food where the board is suitable.

Read more

We discuss a surrounding groove or an additional collecting basin when appropriate. A large format alone does not make a board better; construction, thickness and the way it is used need to work together.

A personal detail for a real occasion

A name, date or restrained motif can make a BBQ board a personal birthday, wedding or housewarming gift. We agree the engraving position before it is made.

Read more

Keep the main cutting area usable and allow space for any groove. If a standard format does not suit the recipient's grill station or kitchen island, we can discuss a made-to-measure board.

A gift made to be used

Wood will show use. A thoughtful gift includes an understanding of cleaning, drying and care, rather than an expectation that the board must remain untouched.

Questions before you choose

What size should a BBQ board be?

Choose around the food, available worktop, handling and cleaning space. We can advise on a suitable existing or custom format.

Can it be engraved?

Yes, subject to the board and motif. We agree a position that works with the working area and any juice groove.

Does every BBQ board need a juice groove?

No. It depends on the food and intended use. We recommend it only where it makes sense for the board.
