Choose a ritual, not just an object

Breakfast, baking, preparing dinner and sharing food each suggest different pieces. A butter knife or dough scraper can be as thoughtful as a large board if it fits the recipient.

Read more

Consider space, weight and how often the piece will be used. A serving board suits a different routine from a permanently placed chopping block. We can help you choose within your budget.

A shared design for the family

Matching breakfast boards or complementary kitchen tools can connect individual gifts without making every piece identical. Natural grain still gives each one its own character.

Read more

We discuss wood, format and personal details together. Availability can vary, so a photo selection is useful where several individual pieces are in stock.

Personalise with care

An individually agreed engraving can add a name, date or short message. We prepare a mock-up before making it and choose a position that suits the product.

Read more

For company gifts, quantity, budget per piece and timing are important from the start. Our dedicated corporate page provides an enquiry route for that discussion.

Know what comes with the gift

The specific product page lists the contents. Extra pieces or care products shown in a photograph are not automatically included with every product.

Read more

Tell us if you would like to combine items. We agree the actual pieces and presentation rather than treating a sample photograph as a universal set.

Questions before you choose

Can a wooden gift be personalised?

Yes, where the piece and motif are suitable. We agree the engraving with you before production.

Do you offer gifts for businesses?

Yes. Tell us the quantity, budget and occasion through our corporate gifts page.

How should the recipient care for it?

Hand wash, dry and allow air to circulate. Follow the care guidance for the specific product.
