Why a board for a kitchen island needs a different approach

A board in an open kitchen does more than cut. It stays in view: on an island, beside natural stone, in a bright kitchen, against dark cabinetry or where guests can see you work. Before choosing, we consider not just wood and price but format, weight, grip, surface, edge and future care.

Some kitchens call for an understated walnut board. Others suit a substantial end-grain board, a 3D pattern or an Erbstück made from historic timber. Not every striking piece suits every room. That is where our advice begins.

What makes the difference visible

The price of a good board is not explained by calling it premium. It makes sense when you can see the material, construction and service behind it.

Selected timber

Understated or distinctive grain is chosen to suit the format.

Considered thickness

Thickness and weight should suit its place: easy to move, permanently on the worktop or used as a chopping block.

Details that fit

Edges, grip recesses, juice grooves and engraving are recommended only when they make sense.

Long-term care

Care and later refurbishment are part of the product's life.

Which kitchens is this selection for?

For people who want to shape their kitchen thoughtfully: contemporary interiors, kitchen islands, open-plan spaces, marble worktops, dark or light cabinetry, entertaining areas and architect-designed homes. And for gifts that feel truly considered.

How an enquiry works

  1. Send your idea. Briefly describe how you want to use it. Dimensions or a kitchen photo can follow later.
  2. Work out the details together. We discuss wood, construction, details, price and production time. You do not need to know everything in advance.
  3. Make it after your approval. We produce the agreed design and arrange delivery or collection.

Service that starts before the board

We want you to know what to expect from a board in this selection. We show you the material, dimensions and details so you can look forward to the finished piece. For available one-offs, we are happy to answer short questions on WhatsApp. Custom projects start with a brief enquiry. We then record the agreed dimensions, materials and details together.

Our aim is a process that matches the care put into the piece: personal, calm and clear. Tell us freely what size or effect you have in mind. We explain what makes technical sense, which woods are available and when a special board will need more time.

Frequently asked questions

A few things to consider before choosing.

How is a board for an open kitchen different from a standard cutting board?

It is considered as a visible part of the room: an open kitchen, island, contemporary interior or entertaining space. Grain, construction, thickness, weight, edge and intended use are chosen together. Edle Hölzer Atelier is our selection and custom-making process for that.

Can I choose the wood and format?

You can share your preferences, but not every format makes sense in every wood. Bring your kitchen plans, worktop dimensions or the effect you want, and we will assess size, thickness, construction and available material for your space.

Why do some boards cost considerably more?

Price differences come from material, thickness, construction, size, work involved, wood selection and details such as a juice groove, engraving or special edges.

Are these boards for daily use or just serving?

They are made to be used and seen: in the kitchen, on an island, when entertaining, at the barbecue or for serving. What matters is that the particular board suits its purpose.
