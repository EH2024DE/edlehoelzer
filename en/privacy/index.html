Privacy notice

English translation of our German privacy notice. This notice describes the technical setup of the website and does not replace a legal review of the applicable legal bases and wording.

Contact us about privacy

1. Controller

Edle Hölzer GbR, Özgür Koç and Serkan Koç, Schulgasse 4A, 35460 Staufenberg, Germany. Email: info@edlehoelzer.de.

2. Access data and server logs

When you access this website, the hosting provider automatically records your IP address, date and time, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL and the pages or files requested. These data are technically necessary to provide the website and ensure its stability and security. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The data are deleted when they are no longer required.

3. Hosting

This website is provided through GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Technically necessary personal data, particularly server logs, are processed when you visit. Processing serves the provision and secure delivery of this website under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Processing on servers in the USA cannot be ruled out. Further information is available in GitHub's privacy statement, linked below.

Consent management and optional services

On your first visit, you can decide whether statistics services and external media may be loaded in addition to technically necessary functions. Your choice is stored locally in your browser so that you do not have to choose again on every page. Without your consent, we do not load statistics, marketing or external-media scripts. You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings in the footer.

Web analytics with Umami and GoatCounter

If you consent to Statistics, we use Umami and GoatCounter for data-minimising audience and intent measurement. This includes page views, referrers, device types and selected interactions such as product views, Etsy clicks, comparisons and contact actions. We do not collect free-text form entries or message contents. Product-related events are technically reduced so that freely entered content is not transmitted. Processing takes place only with your consent, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and, where access to information on your device is involved, the relevant TDDDG provision. Providers: Umami Software, Inc. and GoatCounter. Their privacy notices are linked below.

4. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide to handle your enquiry. The legal bases are Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual steps and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The data are deleted once the enquiry has been completed and no statutory retention obligations remain.

5. Contact form: Formspree

If you contact us through a form on this website, the information entered, such as name, company, email address, telephone number, message and further voluntary details, is transmitted to and processed by Formspree Inc., USA. Processing serves the handling of your enquiry under Article 6(1)(b) and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Data may be processed on servers outside the European Union, particularly in the USA. Formspree states that it uses appropriate safeguards to protect personal data. Its privacy policy is linked below.

6. External links: Etsy

This website contains links to our Etsy shop. Clicking a link takes you away from our website. Etsy is solely responsible for data processing on Etsy.

Pinterest Developer App and Pinterest API

Edle Hölzer may use a Pinterest Developer App or the Pinterest API to manage its own content, product information, pins, boards and analytics more efficiently, including planning, publishing, management and analysis. Depending on use and technical implementation, processing may include Pinterest account IDs or profile information, board and pin information, published content and metadata, API permissions or scopes, OAuth access tokens and, where applicable, refresh tokens, plus technical logs for troubleshooting and security.

OAuth tokens are stored and used only for the specified purposes where necessary to technically provide, automate, manage and analyse Edle Hölzer's own Pinterest activities. They must not appear publicly in frontend code, the repository or client code; they must be stored server-side or in secure secret management. The legal basis is our legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR unless consent or another legal basis applies. Pinterest's privacy policy, terms of service and developer terms also apply; links are provided below.

External media

Some pages may contain embedded videos. These media are loaded only after you consent to the relevant category. Before that, only a local placeholder is displayed. The provider of the currently embedded videos is Google Ireland Limited through YouTube or YouTube-nocookie. Loading a video may transmit personal data to Google.

Google Preferred Sources

Editorial pages offer a button to select Edle Hölzer as a preferred source on Google. The Google library needed for this feature is not loaded when you open the page. Only when you deliberately activate the button is a connection to Google established and the selection dialogue opened. Your IP address and browser and device data may then be transmitted to Google. By clicking, you expressly request this external function. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google's privacy policy is linked below.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access under Article 15 GDPR, rectification under Article 16, erasure under Article 17, restriction of processing under Article 18, data portability under Article 20 where applicable, and objection under Article 21. You can contact us by email at any time. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.
