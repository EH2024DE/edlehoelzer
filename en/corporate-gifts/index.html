FAQ

Questions before your enquiry.

What do an enquiry and individual planning cost?

Your enquiry and an initial price indication based on our existing range are free. If your project requires a new product concept or an individual solution, we agree a one-off concept and planning fee of €150 in advance as the final price. VAT is not shown separately. The scope and cost require separate approval before work begins. If you then commission us to make the same project, the fee is credited in full against that invoice. Without a production order, it pays for the agreed concept work.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Yes. Standard products generally start at 10 pieces. For more customised projects, a sensible quantity is usually 25 pieces or more.

Can you give an exact price straight away?

The exact price depends on quantity, format, engraving and design. That is why we start with realistic price ranges.

Can you engrave a logo?

Yes. Depending on the product, we can incorporate logos and short messages. If needed, we check legibility and appearance beforehand.

Can you accept short-notice orders?

Available products may be adapted at short notice. We check the quantity, customisation and your preferred date before committing.
