What is an end grain cutting board?

In an end grain board, the fibres of the wood stand upright toward the cutting surface. The board is made from many individual pieces arranged so the surface is defined by the end of the grain.

This construction takes more work than a simple long grain board. It also gives the board its visual rhythm, weight and distinctive cutting feel.

When does end grain make sense?

Frequent cuttingUseful when the board is a daily working surface, not a rare serving piece.
Good knivesMany users prefer the feel of upright fibres under the blade.
WeightA heavier board lies calmer, but it also needs a place where it can stay.
CareEnd grain needs drying, oiling and sensible storage like any serious wood board.

When long grain is more practical

End grain is not automatically the best choice. If you need a lighter board, a slim serving board or something you move constantly, a long grain board can be more practical.

FAQ

Common questions

What is an end grain cutting board?

In an end grain board, the wood fibres stand upright toward the cutting surface. This makes the board more complex and usually heavier.

Is end grain better for knives?

Many users find end grain pleasant under the knife, but wood type, care and cutting technique still matter.

Does an end grain board need more care?

It should be kept dry, never put in the dishwasher and refreshed with suitable oil or balm when the surface feels dry.

When is end grain not necessary?

If you want a lighter board for occasional use or mainly serving, a long grain board can be more practical.
