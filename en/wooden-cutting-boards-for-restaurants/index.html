Custom wooden cutting and serving boards for restaurants and hotels

Enquire about your projectExplore the possibilities

Edle Hölzer handcrafts custom solid wood cutting and serving boards in Central Hesse for restaurants, hotels and hospitality concepts. We tailor dimensions, wood species, juice grooves, engraving and details to your use. Other compact wooden solutions are available on request: together, we assess materials, construction and feasibility.

Frequently asked questions

What do an enquiry and bespoke planning cost?

Your enquiry and an initial cost indication based on our existing range are free. If your project requires a new product concept or a bespoke solution to be developed, we agree a one-off concept and planning fee of €150 as the final price in advance. VAT is not shown separately. The scope and fee require separate approval before work begins. If you subsequently commission production for the same project, we deduct the full fee from its invoice. Without a production order, the fee remains payment for the agreed concept work.

Do you make products other than cutting boards?

Yes. We can design custom breakfast, brunch and serving trays. Certain interior alterations may also be possible following consultation. We agree scope, materials, budget and feasibility in advance.

Are small quantities possible?

Yes. We can make single pieces and batches of up to 100. Design, unit price and delivery date are agreed to suit your project and budget.

Can you make custom sizes?

Yes, where the dimensions and construction are practical for the intended use. We agree dimensions, thickness, weight and handling before production.

Can you engrave our logo?

Logo engraving is possible depending on the product and design. We assess size, placement and legibility on the chosen wood before you approve the design.

Can we send you our own idea?

Yes. A description of the idea and intended use is enough for an initial assessment. We can then review dimensions, sketches or photos together.

Are plastic cutting or counter surfaces also possible?

For butchers, hospitality businesses and other food businesses, we also assess custom-cut polyethylene designs, such as PE 500, on request. We agree materials, dimensions and machining for the intended use and your hygiene requirements. Suitable material documentation and technical feasibility are prerequisites.
