# Availability Presentation

- Product cards focus on photography, name, specifications, price and action. No quantities or snapshot dates appear in cards.
- Product details use one concise status: "Im Shop verfügbar", "Für dich auf Bestellung gefertigt", "Aktuell nicht verfügbar" or "Verfügbarkeit im Shop ansehen".
- The positive shop status requires an active available product, positive stock and a public snapshot no older than seven days. It is not a live stock guarantee.
- Made-to-order products receive an enquiry link. The custom XXL board links to the existing custom-board form; other boards use an email enquiry naming the product.
- Variant products explain that available wood and version are selected in the shop. Aggregate stock never implies that every wood is stocked.
- No changes to inventory, pricing, purchase rules, product URLs or checkout.
- Both languages share the same card and status logic. Four-row subgrid preserves price/action alignment without an empty inventory row.

## Reference Principles

Timbermere distinguishes in-stock pieces from made-to-order boards: https://timbermere.com/

e15 routes bespoke work to personal consultation: https://www.e15.com/cms/en/professionals/custom-made-furniture/

The implementation follows these general distinctions, without copying layouts or assets. Technical timestamps remain in the underlying inventory data, not the selection interface.
