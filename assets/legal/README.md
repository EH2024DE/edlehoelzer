Basis: Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1960, Annex I, applicable from
27 September 2026. Commission practical guidelines (April 2026), section 2.3,
illustrate prominent first-click disclosures in the catalogue/header/checkout,
require default-size legibility and recommend a clickable QR destination.

Implementation: visible native disclosure above the catalogue and beside the
product purchase area, including no-JavaScript product pages. Original aspect
ratio, RGB colours, language-specific EU information link and full-size access.
The graphic stays 700px wide for readable text rather than being miniaturised;
narrow screens scroll the image within its own container, not the page.
This is NOT the GARAN commercial durability guarantee label and does not create
an additional manufacturer guarantee.

Scope and remaining review: website placement is not legal certification. The
actual Etsy checkout, order confirmations and other sales channels have not been
changed or certified. Review those separately, including the notice in order
confirmations recommended by the Commission. If a direct checkout is added,
include the notice there and in its order confirmation as well.
